About
Vishal Mishra was born in Unnao, Uttar Pradesh.
He failed his Indian Idol audition as a teenager, but later won Bharat Ki Shaan (Season 3) and was mentored by Lalit Pandit. He debuted as a composer with "Tutak Tutak Tutiya" (2016) and as a singer with "Munna Michael" (2017). "Kaise Hua" from "Kabir Singh" (2019) made him a household name.

About
Shreya Ghoshal was born in Berhampore, Murshidabad, West Bengal.
and grew up in Rajasthan. She won a reality singing
show as a teenager and made her Bollywood debut with
Devdas. Trained in classical music, she is known for her
sweet and powerful voice and sings in many Indian languages.

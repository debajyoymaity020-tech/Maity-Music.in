About
Armaan Malik was born in Mumbai, Maharashtra.
He comes from a musical family, his father is composer Daboo Malik and his brother is Amaal Mallik. He debuted as a child singer with "Bum Bum Bole" in "Taare Zameen Par" (2007), and made his adult playback debut with "Jai Ho" in 2014. His English debut single "Control" came in 2020.

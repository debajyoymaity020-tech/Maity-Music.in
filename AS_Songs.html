About
Arijit Singh was born in Jiaganj, Murshidabad, West Bengal.
He trained in classical vocals, tabla and Rabindra Sangeet, and first became known on the reality show "Fame Gurukul" in 2005. His first Hindi film song was "Phir Mohabbat" ("Murder 2", 2011), and "Tum Hi Ho" from "Aashiqui 2" (2013) made him a star. In 2020 he started his own label, Oriyon Music.

About
Sunidhi Chauhan was born in New Delhi.
She began performing at local gatherings at the age of four, and made her playback debut at thirteen with "Shastra" (1996). "Ruki Ruki Si Zindagi" from "Mast" was her breakthrough, and she is now known for her powerful voice and wide range. She has also judged Indian Idol and coached on The Voice.

About
Jubin Nautiyal was born in Dehradun, Uttarakhand.
He was well known as a singer in his hometown, and
made his playback debut with "Ek Mulaqat" in 2014. He became
a household name with soulful romantic songs like
"Lut Gaye" and "Raataan Lambiyan" over the last decade.

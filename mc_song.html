About
Mohit Chauhan was born in Nahan, Himachal Pradesh.
He studied geology in Himachal, then moved to Delhi and formed the band Silk Route in 1996. Their song "Dooba Dooba" became a big hit. A. R. Rahman then called him to sing "Khoon Chala" in "Rang De Basanti", and he became a leading playback singer.

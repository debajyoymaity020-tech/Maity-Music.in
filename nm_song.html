About
Neeti Mohan was born in Delhi.
She is the eldest of four sisters, and learned music at Gandharva Mahavidyalaya. She won Channel V's "Popstars" in 2003 and joined the band Aasma. Her Bollywood debut came with "Ishq Wala Love" in "Student of the Year" (2012), followed by "Jiya Re" from "Jab Tak Hai Jaan".

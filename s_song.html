About
Shaan (Shantanu Mukherjee) was born in Mumbai, Maharashtra.
He comes from a Bengali musical family and began with jingles and pop albums with his sister Sagarika in 1996. He made his playback debut with "Pyaar Mein Kabhi Kabhi" in 1999, and is also a popular TV host and judge on singing shows.

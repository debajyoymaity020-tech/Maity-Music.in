About
Neha Kakkar was born in Rishikesh, Uttarakhand.
She started singing at jagratas at the age of four, and moved to Mumbai with her brother Tony in 2004. After her first album "Neha The Rockstar" in 2008, "Second Hand Jawaani" from "Cocktail" (2012) became her big breakthrough. She is now one of the most followed Indian artists online.

About
Asees Kaur was born in Panipat, Haryana.
She started singing at the age of five, and her father encouraged her to sing Gurbani. After appearing on "Awaz Punjab Di" and "Indian Idol", she moved to Mumbai and debuted with "Dildara Reprise" ("Tamanchey", 2014). "Raataan Lambiyan" from "Shershaah" (2021) won her a Filmfare Award.

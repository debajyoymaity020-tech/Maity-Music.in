About
Palak Muchhal was born in Indore, Madhya Pradesh.
She began singing on stage as a child, raising money for charity, and made her Bollywood debut with "Damadamm" in 2011. She became famous with "Aashiqui 2" and "Kaun Tujhe". With her brother Palash, she also funds heart surgeries for children through concerts.
